# Site profile: UpFreights (upfreights.com)

## READER
Owners and buyers at small and mid-size businesses that buy from Chinese factories: online sellers (many on Amazon), wholesalers and brands in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and Europe. Many have shipped a few times or are about to ship for the first time, and they read on a laptop at work or a phone between calls. They want to know what it will cost, how long it takes, what paperwork they need and what can go wrong. They are tired of "it depends" answers and of forwarders' sales talk.

## READER IN ONE LINE
a business owner or buyer moving goods from China by sea or air who wants straight answers on cost, time and paperwork

## VOICE
Write to them as "you", the way a well-informed friend in freight would: plainly, warmly, without wasting their time. Explain trade terms (FCL, LCL, CBM, Incoterms) in everyday words the first time. They leave the moment a paragraph sounds like the one before it.

## HOUSE RULES
- Every fact needs a named source. Never write a fact from memory, and drop a fact rather than guess it. (Owner, November 2026.)
- No prices and no transit times unless they come from the company's own rate sheet. (Owner, November 2026.)

## PICTURES
Real cargo, containers, warehouses, packing and paperwork. People may appear at work. No logos of other freight companies. No pictures that suggest a customer, shipment or result we cannot show.

## CONTENT TYPES
None.
